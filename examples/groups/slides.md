Three shapes in one group==.==

<!-- notes -->
The group `row` has three members. Each member's `frame` sets its position. They are 40 apart and centered on one line. The group has no `frame` of its own. To move it, add the same offset to each member's `frame`. The middle card has `constrain-proportions` set to `false`. Its scale along x and along y change independently.
